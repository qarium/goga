# Hook Events for Codegraph and Drift

The hooks platform gives tool packages two levers on the declared view (`goga schema`: per-cell amendment, whole-tree validation) while `goga codegraph` and `goga drift` are extension-free. We decided to extend the single action catalog with six actions delivered entirely by the existing platform — no new mechanism: `codegraph.amend_node` and `codegraph.validate_graph` (hard; symmetric to the schema zone; firing wherever the codegraph routine runs, i.e. also inside every drift run's producer calls), and for drift `drift.run_started` / `drift.run_completed` (soft moments; completion fires on every terminal state, crashes included, carrying the last computed report stage and a finished/crashed outcome with a credential-free reason), `drift.amend_report` (hard list control over the finished scoped report: remove/add entries of a closed shape — exactly kind/cell/types; mutually blind; last adjusting tool in enumeration order wins; exit 0/1 follows the final list), and `drift.amend_entry` (hard per-entry facts amendment over every surviving entry of the final list — comparison-produced and tool-added alike — merged per the schema rules under platform-assigned identities). The reason: one uniform extension model over both views, and a tool-adjustable, fact-carrying drift gate usable as a CI merge gate.

## Considered Options

- **Drift amendment as the sole drift surface with a closed entry shape.** Rejected during the discovery interview: a tool that enriches schema and codegraph nodes with facts would have no way to carry any fact into the drift report — the surface would be list-control only, and the strict shape would make the "extends schema + codegraph + drift" scenario unworkable.
- **The adjusting tool writes `tools` areas itself inside its `amend_report` adjustment.** Rejected: last-wins list control would discard every other tool's facts together with their discarded adjustments, and hand-written identity keys allow one tool to spoof another — a weakening of the merge model every other domain follows.
- **A separate per-entry facts amendment (`drift.amend_entry`).** Chosen: the literal transposition of `amend_cell` / `amend_node` onto report entries — all subscribed tools contribute under platform-assigned identities and merge per the established rules; a tool's cross-domain knowledge travels between its hooks via the per-tool `self` context, not through report fields.

## Consequences

- "Silence" of the drift report is narrowed to an adjustment-provenance ban (no markers, annotations, or flags signaling that an entry was removed, added, or adjusted); the `tools` facts areas are the established goga-wide contribution idiom, appear iff tools contribute, and leave the output byte-identical when no tool is subscribed.
- Drift exit codes keep their meanings; 0 vs 1 is decided by the final entry list alone — facts areas never affect it; hard failures of either amendment action exit 2 with nothing on stdout; a codegraph veto exits 1 (`goga codegraph`) or 2 when it fires inside a drift run, matching the existing schema-gate precedent.
- The PRD in this topic was revised in the same decision (sixth action, redefined silence, strict entry shape, run ordering, success criteria) so later stages do not drift from it.
